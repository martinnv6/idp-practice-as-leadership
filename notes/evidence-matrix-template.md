# Evidence matrix: the six questions a selection panel asks

Template for sprint session s5. Copy it to `notes/private/evidence-matrix.md`, which Git ignores, and fill it with your own career. This is the one exercise that is about you, not Kestrel Parcel.

Rules:

- Only what you can prove. No invented numbers, team sizes or results. If you are not sure of a figure, mark it "approx." or leave it out.
- Each answer has three parts: **what I did** (with a number and its context), **what I have not done** (one sentence, no apology), **how I would do it here** (method and first 90 days).
- Aim for one to two minutes per answer, conclusion first.

## The six criteria

| # | What the panel looks for | Question to rehearse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Measured developer productivity (baseline, DORA-type metrics or cycle time, impact of AI tools) and reported results to senior management | How have you measured the productivity of development teams, and how did you report it upward? |
| 2 | Technical credibility across several stacks, enough to lead a specialist per technology | You will lead specialists in stacks you do not master. How do you earn their trust? |
| 3 | Led AI adoption in development at scale, across teams and contractors, with quantified results | Tell me how you took AI in development to several teams and contractors, and what it achieved. |
| 4 | Managed and evaluated development vendors, with impact on contract decisions, and administered a budget | How do you evaluate a delivery vendor, and what contract decisions came out of it? Have you owned a budget? |
| 5 | Redesigned engineering practices and roles, including hybrid teams with AI agents | How would practices and roles change when AI agents work beside people? |
| 6 | Seniority for a company-wide office that reports to senior management; fluent English | What is the largest initiative you have led, and what did you report to leadership? Answer one part in English. |
