## The scenario in three lines

Kestrel Parcel is a fictional parcel logistics company. Its Developer Platform Group has one head, six platform engineers and three matrix Guild Leads, and serves about 420 developers, a large share of them contractors. The office owns the internal developer platform as a product.

## Rules of the practice

1. Nothing about a real employer or real experience is invented here.
2. Kestrel Parcel numbers are practice figures.
3. Every exercise ends in a committed artefact and an answer said aloud.
