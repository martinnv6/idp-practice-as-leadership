## The scenario in three lines

Forja Norte is a fictional steel producer. Its Software Engineering Office has one head, four platform engineers and four matrix Hub Leaders, and serves about 600 developers, most of them contractors. The office owns the internal developer platform as a product.

## Rules of the practice

1. Nothing about a real employer or real experience is invented here.
2. Forja Norte numbers are practice figures.
3. Every exercise ends in a committed artefact and an answer said aloud.
