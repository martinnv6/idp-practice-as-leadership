# Leadership track

Fourteen exercises. Full instructions: [practice plan](https://github.com/martinnv6/idp-practice-as-leadership/blob/main/leadership/03-practice-plan.md). The practice app presents them as missions and tracks them.

| # | Exercise | Interview question it prepares |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Define the platform and its charter | What is an IDP? What is this office for? |
| L2 | Discovery and baseline | How would you start? |
| L3 | Roadmap and prioritisation | How do you prioritise and say no? |
| L4 | Golden-path plan | What is a golden path and how would you launch one? |
| L5 | Adoption plan | How do you get contractors and established teams to adopt standards? |
| L6 | Measurement with DX Core 4 | How do you measure adoption and impact? |
| L7 | Governance and exceptions | How do you balance mandate and autonomy? |
| L8 | Budget and TCO | How would you justify the investment? |
| L9 | Delivery vendors | How do you measure contractor quality? |
| L10 | AI programme | How do you take AI beyond assistants and measure it? |
| L11 | Team and hiring | How would you structure and hire the team? |
| L12 | Incident and continuity | How do you run post-incident improvement? |
| L13 | Quarterly executive report | What would you show me in twelve months? |
| L14 | Explain the IDP end to end | How does it actually work? |

## Counterpart agents

Role-play partners in `.claude/agents/`: executive committee, developer customer, vendor account manager, finance partner, platform engineer. The `run-mission` skill runs a mission with you; the `review-checkpoint` skill scores your work.
